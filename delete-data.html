Delete Your Data

Snake Arena Multiplayer · Developer: Yves Flückiger (GratWerk)

Snake Arena has no user accounts. Most data (scores, skins, settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

How to request deletion of online data

  1. Send an email to yvesflueckiger@icloud.com with the subject “Snake Arena data deletion” and the player name you use in the game.
  2. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous online ID (Firebase Authentication).
  • Your display name, ghost replays and challenge results stored for online play (Firebase Realtime Database).

What is kept

  • Nothing is kept by us. Match data is already removed automatically when a match ends.
  • Data processed by Google (AdMob, Google Play Games, Google Play Billing) is managed in your Google account: myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.

See also our Privacy Policy.
