Privacy Policy

Snake Arena · Last updated: 21 April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Yves Flückiger ("we", "us", or "the Developer") handles information when you use the mobile game Snake Arena ("the App") on iOS and iPadOS.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Developer does not operate any backend server and does not directly collect, store, or process any personal data from players. In particular, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, or any other personal files.
  • Any analytics, crash logs, or usage statistics transmitted to us.

2. Information Processed On Your Device

The following information is stored only locally on your device and is never transmitted to the Developer:

  • Your chosen display name (used in multiplayer and on leaderboards).
  • Your high scores, achievements, and game settings.
  • In-app purchase status (e.g. whether you have unlocked cosmetic items).

3. Third-Party Services

Snake Arena uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review:

Google AdMob (advertising)

The App displays occasional interstitial advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may process device identifiers (including the Advertising Identifier / IDFA, if you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), IP address, coarse location, and ad interaction data in order to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud.

You may decline IDFA tracking when prompted — the App will continue to work, and you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. Users who purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase will no longer see ads.

Google AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Apple Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your display name and scores may be submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards and achievements service. This is handled entirely by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Apple StoreKit (In-App Purchases)

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The Developer receives only anonymous receipt verification — never your payment details.

4. Local Network & Bluetooth (Multiplayer)

Snake Arena supports 1v1 and 2-player matches over your local Wi-Fi network and Bluetooth using Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework. During a multiplayer session, only game state (snake positions, scores, apple coordinates, and your chosen display name) is exchanged directly between the devices. No multiplayer data is sent to the Developer or to any external server.

5. Permissions Requested

  • Local Network / Bluetooth: required to discover and connect to a nearby player for multiplayer matches.
  • App Tracking Transparency: optional — used by Google AdMob to show more relevant ads.
  • Photo Library (save only): optional — used if you choose to save a screenshot of your final score.

6. Children's Privacy

Snake Arena is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only third-party SDK that processes device-level data is Google AdMob, which is configured in a family-friendly manner. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Data Retention

Because the Developer does not collect personal data, there is no server-side retention. Locally stored data (settings, scores, purchases) remains on your device until you delete the App or manually reset it in the in-app settings.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU/EEA under GDPR and Switzerland under the Federal Act on Data Protection), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, and to object to or restrict processing. Because we do not hold any personal data ourselves, such requests should be directed to the relevant third-party service (e.g. Google, Apple). You may also delete all local data simply by uninstalling the App.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
