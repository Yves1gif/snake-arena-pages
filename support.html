Snake Arena — Support

Need help? You're in the right place.

Snake Arena is a modern remake of the classic Snake game, built as an independent project by Yves Flückiger. If you run into any issues, have questions, or want to send feedback, please use the contact options below.

Contact

When reporting a bug, please include:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro).
  • iOS / iPadOS version (Settings → General → About).
  • App version (shown at the bottom of the in-game Settings screen).
  • A short description of what you were doing and what went wrong.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play multiplayer?

Both players need to be on the same Wi-Fi network (or have Bluetooth enabled). On the first device, open the game and choose Multiplayer → Host. On the second device, choose Multiplayer → Join. Your friend's device should appear in the list within a few seconds.

My friend's device doesn't appear in the multiplayer lobby.

Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network, and that the App has "Local Network" permission enabled (Settings → Snake Arena → Local Network). On older devices, enabling Bluetooth can also help discovery.

I bought "Remove Ads" / a cosmetic item but it's not showing up.

Open Settings inside the game and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to make the purchase.

How do I submit my score to the global leaderboard?

Sign in to Apple Game Center (iPhone Settings → Game Center). Scores are submitted automatically at the end of each solo match.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Single-player and local multiplayer work without an internet connection. An internet connection is only needed for Game Center leaderboards and ad delivery.

How can I reset my progress?

Open the in-game Settings screen and scroll to the bottom. There you will find a "Reset progress" option.

How is my data handled?

Snake Arena does not collect personal data. Please see our Privacy Policy for details.
